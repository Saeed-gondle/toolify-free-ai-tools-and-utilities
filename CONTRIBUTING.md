# Contributing to Toolify Studio

Thank you for your interest in [Toolify Studio](https://toolify.studio)!

## 🌐 Using Toolify Studio

The best way to support Toolify Studio is to use it and share it:

1. **Visit**: [https://toolify.studio](https://toolify.studio)
2. **Explore**: Browse [312+ free tools](https://toolify.studio/tools) across 18 categories
3. **Share**: Tell your friends, colleagues, and communities about Toolify Studio

## 💡 Suggesting New Tools

Have an idea for a new tool? We'd love to hear it!

- **Contact us**: [toolify.studio/contact](https://toolify.studio/contact)
- **Email**: toolifystudio@duck.com

When suggesting a tool, please include:
- What the tool should do
- Who would use it
- Examples of similar tools (if any)

## 🐛 Reporting Bugs

Found a bug in any of our [312+ tools](https://toolify.studio/tools)? Please report it:

1. Go to [toolify.studio/contact](https://toolify.studio/contact)
2. Describe the issue and which tool is affected
3. Include your browser and device info
4. Include screenshots if possible

## ⭐ Spreading the Word

The best contribution you can make:

- **Star this repo** ⭐ — helps with discoverability
- **Share on social media** — Tweet, post on Reddit, write a blog post
- **Recommend to others** — developers, students, marketers, writers
- **Write a review** — on Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, or your blog
